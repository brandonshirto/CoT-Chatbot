Chatbot Pilot Testing Portal

Thank you for participating in our pilot testing phase!

How to Test:

  • Click the chat bubble icon in the bottom corner to launch the bot.
  • Test common queries (e.g., account help, order tracking, FAQs).
  • Try unexpected or complex phrasing to evaluate fallback handling.

Need to report a bug?

If the bot responds incorrectly or gets stuck, note the question you asked and share it with the team.
